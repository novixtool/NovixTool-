About Us

Novix Tool is a collection of simple, fast and easy-to-use online tools designed to make everyday digital tasks easier.

What is Novix Tool?

Novix Tool is an online platform that provides useful tools for working with images, PDFs, QR codes and text.

Our goal is to make common digital tasks simple and accessible without requiring complicated software or technical knowledge.

Our Tools

Novix Tool currently provides a growing collection of browser-based tools, including:

  • Background Remover
  • JPG to PDF Converter
  • Image to PNG Converter
  • Image Compressor
  • QR Code Generator
  • Word Counter

Our Mission

Our mission is to build useful online tools that are straightforward, fast and convenient to use. We aim to continuously improve Novix Tool and add more helpful tools over time.

Simple. Fast. Useful.

We believe online tools should be easy to understand. That's why we focus on clean design, simple interfaces and practical features.
